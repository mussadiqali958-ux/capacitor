Data sources, mapping checks and calculation method

11 kV line-to-line; balanced three-phase linear voltage-drop estimate. Before kW and kVAr are distributed using the workbook’s downstream transformer kVA shares. After kVAr is reduced only on the capacitor-to-source path. Current and conductor I²R losses are recalculated at nominal 11 kV. Voltage changes also reach downstream and neighbouring branches through shared upstream impedance.

Bank output is fixed kVAr in this demo. Real shunt banks vary with voltage squared. Source power excludes calculated line losses. Transformer/LT losses, phase imbalance, regulator actions, harmonics and switching transients are excluded. 0.95–1.05 pu is a user-facing demo screening band, not a verified local utility limit. This is a planning demonstration; confirm survey joins and demand before design.

Stress and light-load inputs are assumptions. July uses the earlier workbook input of 260 A and 0.90 PF; transformer nameplate shares are not measured demand. Existing capacitor equipment is not independently modelled. The search compares one entered bank size across nodes, screening voltage, leading source kVAr and thermal loading; it is not a complete installation optimization.
